# Visual direction research

**Status:** Deliberately not selected. The blueprint requires live research after the backend golden path, two distinct directions, four focused mocks, and explicit owner selection before production UI coding.

## Research contract

The study will review 8–10 current examples across editorial data products, developer infrastructure, observability/incidents, version-control diffs, knowledge lineage, and museum/publication information design. Each record will include URL, date viewed, one specific learned principle, what must not be copied, and an accessibility observation.

The two hypotheses remain separate:

- **Editorial Systems Cartography:** typographic authority, warm neutral surfaces, fine technical rules, graph-as-map, marginal evidence annotation, and one restrained signal color.
- **Precision Instrument Panel:** calm operational density, monochrome foundation, state bands, exact numeric rhythm, minimal depth, and rapid scanning.

Each direction will receive a 1440×900 Command Center mock and Patch Review mock. The owner will select one; the implementation will not indiscriminately blend them.

The final system must exclude generic AI gradients, glowing/mesh ornament, ubiquitous glass cards, default shadcn styling, generic three-card layouts, decorative AI iconography, fake terminals, excessive rounded rectangles, and gratuitous motion.
